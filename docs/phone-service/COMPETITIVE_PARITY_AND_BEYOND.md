## Phantom 'beyond' layer

Parity is the floor, not the product ceiling.

1. **Mastermind persistent organizational memory** — governed memory across customers, operations, decisions, policies, projects, and evidence.
2. **Capability Graph** — explicit model of available capabilities, dependencies, health, permissions, and evidence.
3. **Desired-state reconciliation** — continuously compare intended business/system state with observed state and safely converge where authorized.
4. **Independent verification** — every consequential automation can emit evidence receipts rather than relying on a UI status flag.
5. **Proof-carrying automation** — actions carry authorization, inputs, policy evaluation, execution result, and verification evidence.
6. **Continuous self-diagnosis and recovery** — detect failures, isolate blast radius, recover, verify recovery, and preserve audit history.
7. **Cross-domain orchestration** — business, communications, commerce, infrastructure, security, and AI operations coordinated by one policy-controlled intelligence layer.
8. **Economic intelligence** — continuously model revenue, cost-to-serve, provider costs, margins, funding coverage, and sustainability before enabling expensive automation.
9. **Policy/security plane** — least privilege, secret isolation, tenant boundaries, approval gates, anomaly detection, audit integrity, and fail-closed behavior.
10. **Vendor-neutral boundary** — external providers are replaceable adapters; core orchestration and verification remain first-party.
11. **Portable business state** — customers can export governed data, evidence, configuration, and workflow definitions without vendor lock-in.
12. **Offline/edge resilience** — where device/platform capabilities permit, continue critical workflows during connectivity degradation and reconcile safely afterward.
13. **Continuous competitive intelligence** — periodically benchmark the platform against leading CRM, marketing, automation, agent, payments, scheduling, and business-OS products and turn validated gaps into backlog items.

## Non-negotiable truth model

`PLANNED -> BUILT -> TESTED -> VERIFIED -> DEPLOYED -> PRODUCTION`

External network/service model:

`AUTHORIZED -> PROVISIONED -> CONNECTED -> OBSERVED -> VERIFIED -> LIVE`

No marketing feature, AI agent, payment connector, phone carrier, or satellite provider may be represented as live merely because configuration exists.

## Acceptance standard

A parity capability is not considered complete until it has:
- implementation evidence;
- automated tests where applicable;
- security/authorization checks;
- observable runtime behavior;
- failure-path coverage;
- auditability;
- deployment evidence when production-bound;
- and, for externally dependent capabilities, legitimate provider authorization and observed real-world evidence.
