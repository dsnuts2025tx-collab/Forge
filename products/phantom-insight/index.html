Socrates: the heart of the learning experience

Socrates is not just a name on a menu. He is the critical-thinking mentor at the center of Insight—helping learners examine assumptions, test ideas, consider other viewpoints, and build understanding instead of passively accepting an answer.

“The beginning of wisdom is to examine what we think we know.”

A guiding spirit for the experience: curiosity before certainty, thoughtful questions before conclusions, and reflection that leads to growth.

1
Ask better questions

Clarify the question, define key words, and find out what is actually being claimed.

2
Examine assumptions

Notice what you are taking for granted. Ask what evidence or experience supports it.

3
Reason with care

Separate evidence, interpretation, belief, and speculation. Look for gaps and alternative explanations.

4
Reflect and apply

Connect what you learned to choices, relationships, creativity, and your continuing personal growth.

World traditions

Astronomy & science

Understand the cosmos

Solar system, stars, constellations, galaxies, seasons, observation, celestial navigation, and the difference between scientific models and symbolic systems.

Myth & meaning
