## Core positioning
**TELL FORGE. MAKE IT.**

Forge Power is an AI business and engineering powerhouse built around a simple promise: turn a customer's idea into a working project without requiring them to become a software engineer.

**One tap starts the paid conversion path.** The promotion must never imply that production builds, exports, deployments, or premium engineering value are free.

**MAKE IT NOW ⚡**

2. Choose/confirm the Forge plan
3. Secure Stripe checkout
4. Entitlement activates
5. Tell Forge what to build
6. Forge builds the project
7. Preview / export / deploy according to entitlement

Use the approved **3D holographic Forge Power** visual language: black/deep-navy environment, electric blue and violet energy, translucent holographic surfaces, glowing Forge cube/platform, floating builder/preview/deployment panels, premium cinematic depth, crisp white typography.

## Channel rollout
- Product Hunt: launch story + 30-second demo + founder note
- X: launch thread + short demo clips + build examples
- LinkedIn: founder/business case + customer workflow + engineering story
- YouTube Shorts: 15-second holographic hook + 30-second demo
- Instagram Reels: visual-first 15-second hook + CTA
- TikTok: problem → holographic Forge reveal → build result
- Reddit: transparent build story and useful examples; no spam
- Hacker News: technical architecture story only when the product is genuinely production-proven
- SEO: AI software builder, AI app builder, no-code AI development, AI business software, full-stack AI builder

## Credibility rules
Never claim customer counts, ratings, revenue, production readiness, or customer outcomes unless verified. Never describe an unverified deployment as live. Never promise free production builds when the product is paid.
